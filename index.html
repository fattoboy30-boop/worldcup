World Cup History

A journey through the greatest moments in football

1930

Uruguay 1930

The first-ever FIFA World Cup. Uruguay defeats Argentina 4-2 in the final to become the first champions.

1950

Brazil 1950

The "Maracanazo" - Uruguay shocks Brazil 2-1 in the final match at Maracana Stadium.

1958

Sweden 1958

17-year-old Pele announces himself to the world, scoring twice in the final as Brazil wins.

1966

England 1966

"They think it's all over... it is now!" Geoff Hurst's hat-trick wins it for England at home.

1970

Mexico 1970

Brazil's beautiful football. Pele, Jairzinho, Tostao - the greatest team ever assembled.

1986

Mexico 1986

"Hand of God" and "Goal of the Century" - Diego Maradona's legendary tournament.

1998

France 1998

Zinedine Zidane scores twice in the final as France wins on home soil.

2002

Korea/Japan 2002

First World Cup in Asia. Ronaldo's redemption as Brazil wins fifth title.

2010

South Africa 2010

"Waka Waka" - Africa hosts for the first time. Spain's tiki-taka prevails.

2014

Brazil 2014

Germany's 7-1 demolition of Brazil. The most shocking World Cup match ever.

2022

Qatar 2022

The greatest final ever. Messi vs Mbappe. Argentina wins in penalties. Football at its peak.
